# WowTube help

## Showing and hiding

- Press Ctrl+Alt+Y in the game to show or hide the player. A left-click on the tray icon does the same.
- The first time WowTube starts, a welcome window shows this hotkey and what the game needs. If the game is not running, its "Try it on the desktop now" button shows the player straight away, so you can look around and sign in to YouTube. That lasts until you start the game or quit WowTube. At any other time the player shows only while the game runs.
- The player stays hidden when you launch the game. It appears only when you ask for it.
- It hides when you minimize the game, or when another program is in front of the game on the same monitor. The video keeps playing, so you still hear it. To have it pause instead, tick "Pause the video when you switch away from the game" in Settings. It stays put when you click onto a second monitor.

## Hotkeys

- Ctrl+Alt+Y: Show or hide the player
- Ctrl+Alt+S: Maximize over the game, or go back to your size
- Ctrl+Alt+L: Lock or unlock
- Ctrl+Alt+P: Play or pause
- Ctrl+Alt+Up and Ctrl+Alt+Down: Volume
- Ctrl+Alt+Q: Mute or unmute the video, without changing the volume level
- Ctrl+Alt+F: Video full screen, on and off
- Esc: While the player is maximized, return it to your size

Your keyboard's own media keys work too. If it has a play and pause key, that key pauses and plays the video while you are in the game.

Every hotkey can be changed or turned off in Settings.

## Settings

- Open Settings from the menu button on the player, or by right-clicking the tray icon. It is in two columns: Hotkeys on the left, and on the right the headings Player, YouTube Shorts, When you are away from the game, and WowTube.
- To change a hotkey, click its box and press the new keys together with Ctrl, Alt or Shift. Clear turns that hotkey off. Press Save when you are done.
- If another program already uses a key combination, WowTube tells you when it starts and when you save, and you can pick a different one.
- Settings also holds the player's opacity, in steps of 10%, and these choices: Keep the player inside the game window, hide the bar or the border until your pointer is over the player, show the video's progress and volume on the border, show the video full screen button on the bar, always show the like button on the bar, automatically switch to the tall shape for YouTube Shorts, automatically show Shorts in video full screen, automatically play the next Short when one ends, pause the video when you switch away from the game, show the player only while the game is running, and start with Windows.
- Under the hotkeys, "Reset to defaults" has three buttons: Hotkeys, Options, and Size and player location. Each asks first, then fills in the defaults in the window. Nothing changes until you press Save, and Cancel leaves everything as it was.

## The bar

- Menu button (three lines): Back, YouTube Home, YouTube Shorts, Open copied link, Share: Copy a link to this video, Video full screen, the two bar button choices, Lock, Reset size, the hide options, Settings, What's new, Help, About and Quit WowTube.
- Rest your pointer on a bar button to see what it does.
- Drag the player by any part of the bar.
- Video full screen button (four corners, beside the menu button): One click fills the player with the video alone. There it turns into a bent arrow, and one click brings the YouTube page back. If you bring the page back on a Short, the next Shorts stay on the page until you choose video full screen again. To have the button on the bar only while in video full screen, untick "Show the video full screen button on the bar" in the menu or in Settings.
- Like button (a thumbs-up, beside the menu button): It appears in video full screen, where YouTube's own like button is out of sight, and presses that button for you. It works on Shorts and on regular videos. It is filled in gold on a video you already like, and a second click takes the like back. To keep it on the bar all the time, tick "Always show the like button on the bar" in the menu or in Settings. You need to be signed in to YouTube.
- Home button (a small house) and Shorts button (a tall card with a play mark and a line each side): Go to the YouTube home page or to YouTube Shorts. After Home or Shorts a cover with a turning ring lies over the player until the page has loaded or the Short is ready to play, and the bar says "Loading…". The wait is YouTube bringing the page, usually three to five seconds for Shorts. The cover lifts by itself after ten seconds at most. Share button (a curved arrow): Copies a link to the video or Short on show, and the bar says "Link copied". Nothing is sent anywhere. These three are on the bar when the player is wide enough. On a narrow player, find them in the menu.
- The player's shape: Wide for regular videos and tall for YouTube Shorts. The player picks it by itself, as you move between the two, and each shape keeps its own size and place. If you turn that off in Settings, the menu gains a line, "Tall shape" or "Wide shape", to switch by hand.
- Square button: Maximize. Click it again to go back. Double-clicking the bar does the same.
- X button: Close the player. Ctrl+Alt+Y brings it back.

## Moving and resizing

- Drag the bar to move the player.
- Drag the small gold corner at the bottom right, or any edge of the thin gold border, to resize. You do not need to hit the line exactly. While a video's time line is on show, the bottom edge between the corners jumps through the video instead, so use a bottom corner to make the player taller.
- Reset size in the menu puts the shape on show back to its starting size: Half the game's width for the wide shape, and a Short's shape at a little over a third of the game's height for the tall one.
- By default the player cannot leave the game window. You can change that in Settings.

## Watching

- Click the video to pause and play it. Your keyboard stays with the game, so your character keeps responding. Ctrl+Alt+P and your keyboard's media key do the same without the mouse.
- To type, click a text box such as YouTube's search. Your keyboard goes to the player until you press Enter or Esc, or click elsewhere.
- For video full screen, click the four-corners button, press Ctrl+Alt+F or choose Video full screen in the menu. It fills the player, not your monitor. The same button, key or menu line leaves it. The F key cannot do this, because your keyboard stays with the game. The app remembers it for next time.
- Double-clicking the video does what YouTube normally does: It turns video full screen on and off.
- A Short opens in video full screen by itself, a few seconds after it appears, and fills the player. Roll the mouse wheel over it for the next Short or the last one. Click the bent arrow or press Ctrl+Alt+F to bring back the page, with its like and comment buttons. The page also comes back when you leave Shorts. To turn this off, untick "Automatically show Shorts in video full screen" in Settings.
- The bottom edge of the border is a time line: A bright line fills a dim track as the video plays. Click it, or drag along it, to jump back or forward. The pointer turns into a hand there, and a small tip shows the time a click would jump to. It does nothing while an ad plays. When you change the volume or mute, a line up the right edge shows the level for about two seconds. The bar shows the time beside the name. To turn this off, untick "Show the video's progress and volume on the player's border" in Settings.
- A right-click in the player does nothing. YouTube's right-click menu and the browser's are both turned off.
- When you search or browse while a video plays, YouTube keeps it going in a mini player in the corner. WowTube draws that mini player smaller so you can still see the page. Click the mini player's top left corner to go back to the video, or its ✕ to close it.
- Shorts play one after another: When one ends, the next starts by itself. To have each Short replay until you scroll, untick "Automatically play the next Short when one ends" in Settings.
- To open a link you copied somewhere else, choose Open copied link in the menu.

## Lock

- Lock fixes the player in place and lets your clicks pass through the video to the game.
- Unlock from the menu or with Ctrl+Alt+L.

## The look

- The border hides when your pointer is away. Turn that off in Settings or the menu.
- You can also hide the bar until your pointer is over the player.
- Opacity is in Settings.

## Good to know

- World of Warcraft must be in "Fullscreen (Windowed)" or "Windowed" display mode.
- YouTube's ads play as normal unless you are signed in with YouTube Premium.
- WowTube does not read the game's memory, change the game, or press keys for you.
- If something goes wrong, WowTube keeps a small log on your own PC that helps find the cause. Press Win+R, paste `%AppData%\WowTube\logs` and press Enter, then send the file `wowtube.log`. The log records what the app did, not which videos you watched, and it is never sent anywhere by the app.

## New versions

- WowTube asks GitHub whether a newer version exists when it starts, every 3 hours, and each time you close the game. It sends nothing about you.
- If there is one, a notice appears near the clock, but only when you are out of the game. It never interrupts play. Click the notice to open the download page.
- The tray menu and the player menu also show "New Version … available" until you update. The tray icon blinks for half a minute, then keeps a dot.
- To ask straight away, open About and press Check for updates.
- Nothing is downloaded or installed for you. If you used the installer, download the new WowTube-Setup and run it: It closes the running copy for you and keeps your settings and sign-in. You do not need to uninstall first, and uninstalling removes your settings and sign-in.
- If you run WowTube from the zip, quit WowTube, download the new zip and replace the file. Keep WowTube.exe in the same folder each time: Windows treats a copy in a new folder as a new program, so its icon by the clock would be hidden again and listed twice in the taskbar settings. If you start WowTube while it is already running, a notice near the clock says so, and the copy that was running carries on.
- The check is always on. It cannot be turned off.

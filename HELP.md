# WowTube help

## Showing and hiding

- Press Ctrl+Alt+Y in the game to show or hide the player. A left-click on the tray icon does the same.
- The player stays hidden when you launch the game. It appears only when you ask for it.
- It hides when you minimize the game, or when another program is in front of the game on the same monitor. The video keeps playing, so you still hear it. To have it pause instead, tick "Pause the video when you switch away from the game" in Settings. It stays put when you click onto a second monitor.

## Hotkeys

- Ctrl+Alt+Y: Show or hide the player
- Ctrl+Alt+S: Maximize over the game, or go back to your size
- Ctrl+Alt+L: Lock or unlock
- Ctrl+Alt+P: Play or pause
- Ctrl+Alt+Up and Ctrl+Alt+Down: Volume
- Ctrl+Alt+Q: Mute or unmute the video, without changing the volume level
- Ctrl+Alt+F: Video full screen, on and off
- Esc: While the player is maximized, return it to your size

Your keyboard's own media keys work too. If it has a play and pause key, that key pauses and plays the video while you are in the game.

Every hotkey can be changed or turned off in Settings.

## Settings

- Open Settings from the menu button on the player, or by right-clicking the tray icon.
- To change a hotkey, click its box and press the new keys together with Ctrl, Alt or Shift. Clear turns that hotkey off. Press Save when you are done.
- If another program already uses a key combination, WowTube tells you when it starts and when you save, and you can pick a different one.
- Settings also holds the video's opacity and these choices: Keep the player inside the game window, hide the bar or the border until your pointer is over the video, make a double-click on the video maximize the player, pause the video when you switch away from the game, show the video only while the game is running, check for new versions, and start with Windows.

## The bar

- Menu button (three lines): Back, Open copied link, Video full screen, Lock, Reset size, the hide options, Settings, What's new, Help, About, Hide WowTube and Quit WowTube.
- Rest your pointer on a bar button to see what it does.
- Drag the player by any part of the bar.
- Square button: Maximize. Click it again to go back. Double-clicking the bar does the same.
- X button: Close the player. Ctrl+Alt+Y brings it back.

## Moving and resizing

- Drag the bar to move the player.
- Drag the small gold corner at the bottom right, or any edge of the thin gold border, to resize. You do not need to hit the line exactly.
- Reset size in the menu puts the player back to half the game's width.
- By default the player cannot leave the game window. You can change that in Settings.

## Watching

- Click the video to pause and play it. Your keyboard stays with the game, so your character keeps responding. Ctrl+Alt+P and your keyboard's media key do the same without the mouse.
- To type, click a text box such as YouTube's search. Your keyboard goes to the player until you press Enter or Esc, or click elsewhere.
- For a video-only view, press Ctrl+Alt+F or choose Video full screen in the menu. It fills the player, not your monitor. The same key or menu line leaves it. The F key cannot do this, because your keyboard stays with the game. The app remembers the view for next time.
- Double-clicking the video does what YouTube normally does: It turns the full-screen view on and off. If you tick "Double-clicking the video maximizes the player" in Settings, a double-click fills the game and another puts the player back, and the video stays in its full-screen view.
- To open a link you copied somewhere else, choose Open copied link in the menu.

## Lock

- Lock fixes the player in place and lets your clicks pass through the video to the game.
- Unlock from the menu or with Ctrl+Alt+L.

## The look

- The border hides when your pointer is away. Turn that off in Settings or the menu.
- You can also hide the bar until your pointer is over the player.
- Opacity is in Settings.

## Good to know

- World of Warcraft must be in "Fullscreen (Windowed)" or "Windowed" display mode.
- YouTube's ads play as normal unless you are signed in with YouTube Premium.
- WowTube does not read the game's memory, change the game, or press keys for you.
- If something goes wrong, right-click the tray icon and choose Open log folder. The log records what the app did, not which videos you watched.

## New versions

- WowTube asks GitHub whether a newer version exists when it starts and every 3 hours. It sends nothing about you.
- If there is one, a notice appears near the clock, but only when you are out of the game. It never interrupts play. Click the notice to open the download page.
- The tray menu and the player menu also show "New Version … available" until you update. The tray icon blinks for half a minute, then keeps a dot.
- To ask straight away, open About and press Check for updates.
- Nothing is downloaded or installed for you. Quit WowTube, download the new zip and replace the file.
- To stop the check, untick "Check for new versions" in Settings.
